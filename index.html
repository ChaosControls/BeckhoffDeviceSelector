E-Bus Segment Planner

List the EL terminals behind each EK coupler in rail order. The planner adds up E-bus current, flags any segment that runs past its supply, and marks where an EL9410 should go to refresh it.

How the budget is counted. Each coupler (or CX controller) feeds the E-bus terminals to its right. An EL9410 counts as a fresh 2,000 mA for the terminals after it, and the coupler's leftover current is not carried past it. That is the conservative reading. "Reserve" holds back a share of each supply so the segment isn't planned right up to the limit.

Current values. The catalog uses typical E-bus draws from Beckhoff datasheets. Values marked verify are less certain. Edit any draw in the table, and compare against the E-Bus column in TwinCAT after scanning the real hardware.
