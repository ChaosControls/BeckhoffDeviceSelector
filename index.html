E-Bus Segment Planner

Start each segment by choosing its bus coupler, then add devices to the fieldbus in rail order. The planner adds up E-bus and K-bus current, flags any segment that runs past its supply, and marks where an EL9410 (or KL9400) should go.

How the budget is counted. The coupler feeds the E-bus terminals to its right. An EL9410 counts as a fresh 2,000 mA for the terminals after it, and the coupler's leftover is not carried past it. A BK1250 starts a K-bus section for KL terminals, refreshed by a KL9400. EP boxes hang off the EtherCAT cable and draw no E-bus current. "Reserve" holds back a share of each supply.

Current values. Draws are typical values from Beckhoff datasheets. Values marked verify are estimates for that product family. Edit any draw in the table, and compare against the E-Bus column in TwinCAT after scanning the real hardware.
